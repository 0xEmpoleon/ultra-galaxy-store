005 // SITEMAP

Pages

/buy (Default)

Token swap interface. USDC → USDai on Arbitrum. Buy/Redeem sub-tabs. Metrics bar (TVL, APY, Exp. APY, Fair Value). Two-step tx flow (Approve + Buy). Allo rewards badge.

/stake

USDai ↔ sUSDai staking. Stake/Unstake sub-tabs. 1:1 exchange rate. 15-day average unstaking notice. Two-step tx flow (Approve + Stake).

/bridge

Cross-chain bridge. Arbitrum ↔ Plasma. USDai + sUSDai tokens. Info banner: "Bridging can take up to 15 minutes." Two-column from/to layout with chain selectors.

/rewards

Allo™ opportunities table. 100+ DeFi integrations across Euler, Pendle, Morpho, Curve, Fluid, Silo, Equilibria, Gamma, Aerodrome, Balancer, Gearbox, Spectra, and more. Columns: Protocol, Network, Type (MM/DEX/Yield), Allo Rate, TVL, APY. Networks: Arbitrum, Plasma, Base, Ethereum.
